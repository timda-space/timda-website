The Executive Suites occupy the finest real estate in the dome. Technocratic Incorporated's leadership thanks you for your interest, and for not visiting.

The Board reviews every docking request personally. Resolutions are carried by signature. Arrivals and departures are announced on the Arrivals board.

Board resolutions

For the hub's operator. A resolution is a statement signed with a key from _key._timda.timda.space; anyone else's is refused.

Out of character

This is the governance desk. New Sections that dock wait here for review: nothing of theirs (name, links, button, banner) appears on this hub until the operator approves them. The operator can also delist a Section at any time, which takes it off the hub at once. See the charter for the rules.

How to table a motion

Write a statement with one of these types, sign it with the hub's key, and upload both above:

cat > motion.txt <<EOF
timda-statement: 1
type: board-approve
from: timda.space
hub: timda.space
issued: $(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)
nonce: $(head -c 16 /dev/urandom | base64 | tr '+/' '-_' | tr -d =)
section: section76.net
EOF
ssh-keygen -Y sign -f <hub key> -n timda motion.txt
  • board-agenda: what's waiting for review, approved and delisted (no section: needed).
  • board-approve: list a Section that's waiting.
  • board-delist: take a Section off the hub at once, and refuse its dock requests. Add reason: if you like.
  • board-reinstate: undo a delisting.

Or, with the timda tool: timda board agenda, timda board approve section76.net, and so on, from a folder whose section.toml is the hub's.
