Wormhole Post

Powered by TWIRL, the Technocratic Wormhole-Integrated Relay Link. Postage: free, pending review by the Board.

Signed mail between docked Sections, sent through the mast and held here for 30 days until it's collected. Hand the relay a signed statement: a message to send, an inbox to collect your mail, or an inbox-delete to clear it.

Open an inbox

Publish one more record, naming this hub. Add your age public key too, and senders must encrypt to it, so not even the hub can read your mail. Without one, it can. The record planner below will write it for you.

_inbox._timda.section76.net.  300 IN TXT "v=TIMDA1; hub=timda.space; age=age1…"

Mail is only carried between Sections docked and approved here. Delete the record to close your inbox. Mail already held stays yours to collect until it expires.

Send a message

 
cat > msg.txt <<EOF
timda-statement: 1
type: message
from: section72.net
to: section76.net
hub: timda.space
issued: $(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)
nonce: $(head -c 16 /dev/urandom | base64 | tr '+/' '-_' | tr -d =)
subject: The modem on bench 3

Your station keeps answering our outside line. Is that you?
EOF
ssh-keygen -Y sign -f ~/.ssh/<your key> -n timda msg.txt

Then upload msg.txt and msg.txt.sig above. The subject is optional, up to 100 characters; the whole message, at most 8 KiB.

If the recipient's inbox record has an age key, encrypt the text to it, and add a header saying so:

subject: The modem on bench 3
encrypted: age

$(echo 'Your station keeps answering our outside line.' | age -a -r age1…)

Or, with the timda tool: timda mail send section76.net --subject "The modem on bench 3". It opens your editor, and seals the message with age by itself if the recipient asks.

Collect and clear your mail

 
cat > inbox.txt <<EOF
timda-statement: 1
type: inbox
from: section76.net
hub: timda.space
issued: $(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)
nonce: $(head -c 16 /dev/urandom | base64 | tr '+/' '-_' | tr -d =)
EOF
ssh-keygen -Y sign -f ~/.ssh/<your key> -n timda inbox.txt

Upload both above and your mail is shown, with each message as it was signed, so you can check the sender yourself. Reading never deletes. To clear some, make the same statement with type: inbox-delete and a line delete: 2f6c1e0a9b, 7d41aa03c2 (message IDs, or all).

Or, with the timda tool: timda mail collects your mail, checks every signature against DNS, and opens sealed mail with your age key; timda mail delete all clears it.

Out of character

This is the signal desk: Sections speak by signing statements with a key published in their own DNS records, and anyone can check a signature against DNS with nothing but ssh-keygen. No accounts, no names.

It's also the beacon listener: the hub re-checks a docked Section's records whenever it lists or routes to it, reusing each check for no longer than the records' TTL, and lists or delists it to match.

The Wormhole Post is TIMDA-3: messages are signed statements, held by the hub until the recipient signs for them. The hub keeps each one exactly as signed, so the recipient can check the sender against DNS without trusting the hub. With an age key, the hub can't read them either; it still knows who wrote to whom, and when.

The beacon test is timda finger in your browser, and the record planner is timda plan. The signal test runs the same checks the hub runs on a dock request, without docking anything. Make a statement to test with ssh-keygen -Y sign -n timda; any statement type works.
