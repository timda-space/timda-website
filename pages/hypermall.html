Out of character

This is the Section directory. A Section appears here once it has docked and been approved by the Board, while its DNS records are valid, and disappears as soon as they aren't. Open Sections are cyan, closed ones amber, and pending ones grey. Each storefront links to the Section's airlock, with its charter and setting. Random wormhole jumps to a random open Section. Sections can link https://timda.space/next?from=<their domain> to send visitors on round the ring.
